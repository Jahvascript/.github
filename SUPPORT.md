# JahvaScript Community Support

## Questions and Technical Help

For technical questions, feature requests, and bug reports,
open an issue in the relevant JahvaScript project repository.

Beginners are welcome. Questions asked in good faith
deserve respectful responses.

## Private Community Reports

To report harassment, discrimination, or potential
Code of Conduct violations, contact:

jahvascripts@gmail.com

Please do not post sensitive reports in public issues.

Reports will be reviewed by the JahvaScript maintainer,
with confidentiality respected as far as reasonably possible.

For security vulnerabilities, please avoid public disclosure
and contact the maintainer privately.
