# Contributing to JahvaScript

## Everyone Is Welcome

JahvaScript welcomes contributors of all backgrounds,
identities, experience levels, and abilities.

You do not have to be an expert to contribute.

You can help by:
- Reporting bugs and suggesting improvements.
- Writing or improving documentation.
- Improving accessibility and usability.
- Contributing code, tests, designs, or translations.
- Helping beginners learn and participate.

## Getting Started

1. Read the project's README and license.
2. Check existing issues and pull requests.
3. Open an issue to discuss substantial changes.
4. Fork the project and create a focused branch.
5. Submit a pull request explaining your changes.

Small contributions are valuable too.

## Community Expectations

Be respectful, constructive, patient, and welcoming.

Do not submit private information, credentials,
proprietary materials, or content you lack rights to share.

Respect upstream licenses and contributor attribution.

Read our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Our Philosophy

Different backgrounds. Different experiences.
One community.

Everyone deserves the opportunity to learn, create,
collaborate, and contribute.
